Common questions

I can't sign in or didn't get my verification code.
Check your spam folder, then use "Resend code" in the app. If it still doesn't arrive, email us from your registered address.

I'm not receiving notifications.
Make sure notifications are enabled for Kafe' 54 in your phone's Settings, then sign out and sign back in.

How do I delete my account?
Go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account in the app, or see our account deletion page.

How do I report a user or content?
Use the Report option on any profile, post, comment or message, or email us.
