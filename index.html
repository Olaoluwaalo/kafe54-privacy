Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026
Last updated: October 28, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how K54 Global ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, shares and protects your information when you use the Kafe' 54 mobile application (the "App") and our website at https://k54global.com (together, the "Services"). By using the Services you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

1.1 Information you provide

  • Account information: name, username, email address, password, profile photo and any other profile details you add.
  • Verification data: one-time passcodes (OTP) sent to your email to verify your account or reset your password.
  • Content you create: posts, comments, messages, photos, videos and other content you share in the App.
  • Communications: messages you send to us for support.

1.2 Sign-in with Google

If you choose "Continue with Google", we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google, according to the permissions you approve. We do not receive your Google password.

1.3 Information collected automatically

  • Device and app information: device model, operating system and version, app version, language, and unique identifiers such as the Firebase installation ID and push notification token.
  • Usage and diagnostic data: crash reports, error logs and performance information (see Firebase Crashlytics below).
  • Approximate location: if you grant permission, we may use your location to show local weather or location-relevant content. You can disable this in your device settings at any time.
  • Log data: IP address, request times and similar technical data recorded by our servers.

2. Third-party services we use

The App uses the following services, which may process data on our behalf:

  • Firebase Crashlytics (Google): collects crash reports and diagnostic data (device type, OS version, app state at the time of the crash, installation identifier) so we can find and fix bugs. Learn more: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.
  • Firebase Remote Config (Google): lets us change app settings and features without releasing an update. It uses a Firebase installation ID to deliver configuration to your device.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google): delivers push notifications (for example new messages or activity). It uses a device push token. You can turn notifications off in your device settings.
  • Google Sign-In: optional sign-in method, governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • WordPress / BuddyBoss platform (our own servers): our website and App run on a WordPress and BuddyBoss backend, accessed through a secure REST API. Your account, profile, social activity, messages and content are stored there.
  • Hosting provider: our servers are hosted by Hostinger, which processes data on our behalf to keep the Services available.
  • Video streaming (WpStream): used to deliver live channels and video content in the App.

These providers have their own privacy policies, and we encourage you to review them.

3. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and authenticate you securely.
  • To provide App features such as profiles, messaging, events, community content and live streaming.
  • To send verification codes, service emails and push notifications.
  • To monitor stability, fix crashes and improve performance.
  • To enable, test and roll out features using Remote Config.
  • To prevent fraud, spam and abuse, and to keep the community safe.
  • To respond to support requests and comply with legal obligations.

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only:

  • with the service providers listed in Section 2, to operate the Services;
  • with other users, to the extent you choose to make content or your profile visible to them;
  • to comply with the law, legal process or a valid government request;
  • to protect the rights, safety and security of our users, the public or K54 Global;
  • in connection with a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, in which case we will notify you of any change in how your data is handled.

5. Data security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), token-based authentication and secure storage of credentials on your device. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Data retention

We keep your information while your account is active and as long as needed to provide the Services. When you delete your account, we delete or anonymise your personal data within a reasonable period, except where we must retain it for legal, security or fraud-prevention purposes. Crash and diagnostic data held by Firebase is retained according to Google's retention policies.

7. Your rights and choices

  • Access and correction: you can view and update your profile information in the App.
  • Account and data deletion: you can delete your account from within the App (Settings), or request deletion by emailing us at the address below. See also our account deletion page.
  • Notifications: disable push notifications in your device settings.
  • Location: withdraw location permission in your device settings.
  • Other rights: depending on where you live (including under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and similar laws), you may have the right to object to or restrict processing, request a copy of your data, or lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

8. Children's privacy

The Services are not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

9. International data transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than your own, including by Google (Firebase) and our hosting provider. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for such transfers.

10. Cookies on our website

Our website uses cookies to keep you signed in, remember your preferences and secure your session. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will post the new version here and update the "Last updated" date. Significant changes may also be communicated in the App.
