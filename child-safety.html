Child Safety Standards

Last updated: October 28, 2026

K54 Global, the operator of the Kafe' 54 app, is committed to protecting children and maintaining a safe community. We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM) on our platform.

1. Prohibited content and behaviour

The following are strictly prohibited anywhere in Kafe' 54, including profiles, posts, comments, groups, messages, photos, videos and live content:

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) in any form
  • Sexualised content involving minors, including images, text, drawings or AI-generated content
  • Grooming, solicitation or any attempt to contact minors for sexual purposes
  • Sextortion, trafficking or any other exploitation of children
  • Sharing, requesting or linking to any of the above

2. Age requirement

Kafe' 54 is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly allow children below that age to hold accounts, and we remove accounts we identify as belonging to underage users.

3. How to report

  • In the app: use the Report option on any profile, post, comment, message or group to flag content for review.
  • By email: contact our child safety team at WAlonge@a7pinc.com with the username, link or screenshot of the concern.

If a child is in immediate danger, contact your local law enforcement or emergency services first.

4. How we respond

  • Reports of CSAE or CSAM are prioritised and reviewed by our moderation team.
  • Violating content is removed immediately and the responsible accounts are suspended or permanently banned.
  • We preserve relevant evidence as required by law and report confirmed CSAM to the relevant authorities, including the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) and local law enforcement agencies where applicable.
  • We cooperate with valid legal requests from law enforcement.

5. Prevention

  • Our Terms of Service and community rules ban CSAE, and enforcement applies to all users.
  • We moderate user-generated content and respond to user reports.
  • We review and update our safeguards and moderation processes regularly.

6. Legal compliance

We comply with applicable child safety laws and regulations in the countries where we operate, including laws on reporting and removing CSAM.
