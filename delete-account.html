Delete Your Kafe' 54 Account

This page explains how to request deletion of your Kafe' 54 account and the data linked to it. The app is operated by K54 Global.

Option 1: Delete from inside the app

  1. Open the Kafe' 54 app and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account.
  3. Confirm the deletion when prompted.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, email us at [YOUR SUPPORT EMAIL] from the email address registered on your account, with the subject "Delete my Kafe' 54 account". Include your username so we can find your account. We will confirm your identity and process the request within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account and login details (name, username, email address, password)
  • Your profile information and profile photo
  • Your posts, comments and messages
  • Your push notification token and device identifiers linked to your account

What may be kept

  • Records we must keep for legal, security or fraud-prevention reasons, kept only as long as necessary.
  • Anonymised crash and diagnostic data held by Firebase, which is retained according to Google's retention policies and is not linked to your identity once your account is deleted.
  • Backups, which are overwritten on a regular cycle, usually within 90 days.

Timeline

Your account is deactivated immediately after the request is confirmed, and your personal data is permanently deleted within 30 days, except for the items listed above.

For details on how we handle your data, read our Privacy Policy.
